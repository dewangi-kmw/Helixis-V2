The Automation Engine, in action

Same event, two very different responses.

A temperature sensor on a telecom tower battery starts climbing. Here's what happens at the device level — and what happens once the automation engine takes over.

Device level · Firmware

  • —Sensor detects rising temperature
  • —Firmware turns on the cooling fan
  • —Load is reduced automatically
  • —An alert signal is sent

Local and instant — but it has no visibility into ERP, technicians or business impact.

Platform level · Automation Engine

  1. 1Checks severity — critical or non-critical
  2. 2Creates a service ticket
  3. 3Assigns the nearest technician
  4. 4Checks spare battery availability in ERP
  5. 5Notifies the operations team
  6. 6Escalates if unresolved in two hours

Cross-system and business-aware — this is automation with consequences that matter.
